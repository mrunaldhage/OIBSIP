A. P. J. Abdul Kalam

Missile Man of India

Dr. A. P. J. Abdul Kalam was an Indian aerospace scientist and the 11th President of India. He is widely known as the “Missile Man of India” for his work in the development of ballistic missile and launch vehicle technology.

He inspired millions of students through his vision, simplicity, and dedication to education and nation-building. His life teaches us to dream big and work hard to achieve those dreams.

“Dream, dream, dream. Dreams transform into thoughts and thoughts result in action.”
